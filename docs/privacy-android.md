# Luminux for Android — Privacy Policy

_Effective 29 October 2026_

Luminux is a photo gallery for the photos and videos on your Android phone. Your photos never leave your phone, and the app has no account. Only three things leave the device: the place lookups, which you can turn off; Google Play's own purchase system; and what an ad needs, only when you choose to watch one.

## Your photos and videos

- **Your library stays on your phone.** Luminux shows, organises, favourites, edits and deletes the photos and videos on your device, all on the device. Nothing is uploaded. If you allow access to only some photos, it sees only those.
- **Edits are saved as a new copy.** Edits are saved as a new photo next to the original, which is never changed. Some editing features are part of Luminux Pro.
- **Deleting** uses Android's own confirmation, and deleted items go to the system trash first.
- **The journal stays on your phone.** Luminux groups your photos into moments, days and trips on the device. Notes and titles you add are stored inside the app and are removed when you uninstall it.
- **The home screen widget** shows small copies of your photos, stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

## Place names in the journal

To title trips like "a day in goa", Luminux uses Android's geocoder, provided by Google Play services, to turn the locations of your photos into place names. Only the locations are sent, never the photos, and the names are kept on your phone. Google handles these requests under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn this off in Settings → "place names".

## Ads

Slideshow, editing and rotating are part of Luminux Pro. Without Pro, you can use one of them once by watching a short ad, or unlock Pro for good with a one-time purchase. Ads only appear when you choose to watch one.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK starts only when you first tap "watch an ad", so if you never do, or you have Luminux Pro, it never runs. Luminux always asks for non-personalised ads and doesn't use your advertising ID.

When an ad loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects:

- a device identifier (the app set ID, not the advertising ID)
- your approximate location, estimated from your IP address
- information about the ads you see and how you interact with them
- performance and crash data from the ad SDK

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Your choices**

- **Consent in the EU, UK and similar regions.** Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad loads. You can change them later in Luminux under Settings → ad privacy choices.
- **No ads at all.** With the one-time Luminux Pro purchase, every feature works without an ad.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, and we never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether Luminux Pro has been bought, so it can be restored on your other devices. Google Play's billing library also sends Google a small amount of diagnostic data about the purchase system when the app opens.

## Children

Luminux isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.
