- [x] **Journal (2026-10-07): photos organised into outings, days and trips, like Apple's Journal suggestions.**
  - `Core/Journal/JournalBuilder.swift` (pure, tested): screenshots are left out. A new moment starts after a 3-hour gap or about 25 km of travel. Moments of 3+ photos become entries; stray photos make a "day" entry only on a day with nothing else. Home is the ~11 km cell photographed on the most different days (photo counts would let a busy trip win). Two or more consecutive days mostly more than 50 km from home (one empty day allowed) become a trip. Entry ids are the first photo's id, so renames and notes survive rebuilds.
  - Titles (`JournalTitles`): "saturday afternoon", "afternoon in goa", "a day in goa", "trip to jaipur" or "4 days away" without a place name; subtitle "friday, 2 october 2026 · 8 photos" or a date range.
  - Place names: only for entries away from home, from Apple Maps (`MKReverseGeocodingRequest` on iOS 26+, `CLGeocoder` before), one lookup at a time 1.2 s apart (Apple throttles), cached per ~5 km cell in `Application Support/journal.json`. Setting "name places in the journal" (on by default) turns it off and hides cached names. The permission screen and the photo-library usage string no longer say "nothing leaves your device"; they say photos never leave it, and only locations are sent to Apple Maps to name places.
  - Key photos: favourites first, then Vision's on-device aesthetics score (`CalculateImageAestheticsScoresRequest`) over up to 12 photos spread through the entry; utility shots (receipts, documents) go last. Scored when an entry's tile appears.
  - Screens: a "journal" hub panel (after "what's new") with the latest 3 entries and "see all"; a journal page by year; an entry page with the title, dates, note and the entry's photos (`AssetSource.identifiers`, oldest first), with "rename" (empty resets to the automatic title) and "note" (sheet editor) in the app bar. Notes and titles stay on the phone in `journal.json`. Free, not Pro.
  - Rebuilds 1 s after any library change, off the main thread.
